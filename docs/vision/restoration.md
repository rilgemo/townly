# Restoration

## Player Experience

The player discovers that Willow Village was not always diminished. Its residents remember warm hearths supplied by the forest, stone drawn from the mine, and buildings that once served a larger community.

Gathering is not performed to increase abstract levels. The player brings back material that can make a lost village future possible again.

## World Change

At this foundation stage, the world changes first through understanding:

- An overgrown trail becomes the old Forest working path.
- A collapsed opening becomes the remains of a Mine that built much of the village.
- Wood, Herb, and Stone become materials connected to restoration rather than collectible numbers alone.

Later restoration should turn those materials into visible changes in Willow Village.

## NPC Philosophy

Residents are witnesses of the village's past, not progression switches.

The Old Woodsman remembers the village's relationship with the forest. The Former Miner remembers what the abandoned tunnels contributed and why that work ended. They provide history, concern, and guidance.

The player observes the paths, enters the places, and gathers the materials. NPCs do not grant mechanical permission and do not gather resources on the player's behalf.

## Completion Test

Restoration Foundations succeeds when:

- Forest still provides Wood and Herb.
- Mine still provides Stone.
- The player can recognize and enter both places through observation.
- Speaking with either resident adds meaning but is not required as an unlock gate.
- The player understands that gathered materials may bring something in Willow Village back.
