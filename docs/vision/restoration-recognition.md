# Restoration Recognition

## Player Experience

After mending the Town Hall door, the player should feel that the change belongs to Willow Village rather than existing only in saved state.

The village does not celebrate with a completion banner. People simply notice that something long neglected now works again.

## World Change

The same repair is recognized from several perspectives:

- Town Square feels less forgotten when the straightened door is visible across it.
- Town Hall remains old, but its entrance closes normally and the building feels usable.
- The Village Chief recognizes care returning to the hall.
- The Old Woodsman sees a small sign that Willow Village can change.
- The Former Miner reads the repair as evidence that the village is worn, not abandoned.

No reaction grants progress, reputation, relationship points, or another feature. Recognition is part of the restoration itself.

## Restoration Creates Memory

A persistent world change should become part of how the village describes itself.

Before the repair, residents have learned to live beside a door that does not close. Afterwards, the working door becomes a shared observation. The village remembers both conditions because the player experienced the difference.

Future restoration recognition should follow the same pattern:

1. A damaged or absent part of village life is visible.
2. The player's concrete actions change it.
3. Places and residents naturally reflect the new reality.
4. The change remains present without requiring an achievement notice.

## NPC Reactions

NPC reactions are observations, not progression rewards.

They should:

- sound consistent with the resident's history and concerns
- acknowledge a visible change rather than praise task completion
- reinforce what restoration means for Willow Village
- use direct state variations without relationship values or dialogue infrastructure

## Completion Test

Restoration Recognition succeeds when:

- Town Square and Town Hall describe meaningfully different conditions after repair.
- The Village Chief, Old Woodsman, and Former Miner each react in their own voice.
- No text announces restoration completion, an achievement, reputation, or an unlock.
- No new state beyond `townHallDoorRepaired` is required.
- Persistence continues remembering the repaired world state.
