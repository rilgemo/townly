# Daily Presence Foundation

## Player Experience

The player should feel that staying in Willow Village matters even when no task is completed and no resource is gained.

Townly focuses on belonging, not task completion. Returning to the square, resting in the shelter, observing ordinary work, and speaking with familiar residents should make the player feel increasingly present in village life.

## World Change

This milestone does not create a simulated day. It changes how existing places and people acknowledge the player's continued presence.

- The shelter offers quiet moments to observe, rest, and reflect.
- The Town Square reveals ordinary life through repeatable observation.
- Residents respond differently when the player has met people or repaired the Town Hall door.
- Existing restoration appears inside daily activity rather than as a completion announcement.

## Presence Without Simulation

Daily presence is not a clock, schedule, routine engine, or checklist.

It is conveyed through small narrative signs:

- cooking smoke and shared food
- familiar faces continuing their work
- village sounds reaching the shelter
- a repaired door being used without ceremony
- residents recognizing that the player is no longer merely passing through

These observations may repeat. Their purpose is to ground the player in a place, not to generate rewards.

## Shelter

The shelter remains empty and mechanically simple.

Its available interactions express living:

- observe the room
- rest against the wall
- reflect on Willow Village

Reflection changes according to existing knowledge and restoration state. It does not track mood, grant bonuses, advance time, or add belongings.

## Town Square

The square is where village continuity becomes visible. Watching it should reveal mutual help and subtle differences after restoration.

The repaired Town Hall door matters because people can use it normally again. The observation is part of village life, not a reward message.

## Familiar Residents

Repeated conversation may acknowledge major existing state:

- whether the player has met residents connected to the village's past
- whether the Town Hall door has been repaired
- whether the player has begun to understand Willow Village

These variations are direct state-based text. They do not imply friendship values, reputation, schedules, dialogue trees, or quests.

## Design Boundary

Daily Presence Foundation does not add:

- time or day simulation
- NPC schedules
- relationship or reputation values
- quests or recurring tasks
- mechanical rest benefits
- new progression systems

Future presence work should first ask whether it makes the player feel more at home. If it only adds another activity counter, it does not belong in this foundation.

## Completion Test

Daily Presence Foundation succeeds when:

- The player can spend a moment in the shelter without opening a system menu.
- The Town Square communicates ongoing life through observation.
- Existing residents acknowledge meaningful existing changes.
- No interaction exists only to dispense progress.
- Willow Village feels like somewhere the player lives, not somewhere they visit to complete tasks.
