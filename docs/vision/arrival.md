# Arrival

## Player Experience

The player begins as a stranger beside an unfamiliar road. Through observation and conversation, an unknown place becomes Willow Village: a small community whose people are cautious but willing to offer shelter.

The sequence should feel like arriving somewhere, not completing a tutorial.

## World Change

The world itself does not suddenly appear. The player's relationship to it changes:

1. An unfamiliar road becomes a road leading to a guarded gate.
2. The guard gives the unknown settlement its name: Willow Village.
3. The closed gate opens and the village becomes somewhere the player may enter.
4. The Village Chief offers temporary shelter.
5. The village changes from an observed place into somewhere the player may begin to belong.

## Opening Sequence

```text
Unknown Road
  → Look around
Village Gate
  → Speak with the Village Guard
Willow Village
  → Enter the village
Town Square
  → Visit the Town Hall
Village Chief
  → Accept temporary shelter
```

## Completion Test

After Arrival, the player should understand:

- The settlement is called Willow Village.
- A Village Guard watches its gate.
- A Village Chief has allowed the player to stay.
- Temporary shelter is available.
- The people around the village may offer ways to help.

The interface must not describe any of these facts before the player observes or learns them.
