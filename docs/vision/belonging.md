# Belonging

## Player Experience

After learning where they are, the player begins to understand why they might stay. Willow Village does not reward the player with property; it makes room for them.

The player should feel recognized before they feel important.

## World Change

The village begins acknowledging the player's presence:

- An unused room becomes **Your Shelter**.
- The Village Guard recognizes the person who now lives near the square.
- The Village Chief speaks differently as the player becomes familiar with residents and helps restore the village.

These changes are direct state-based variations. They are not relationship levels, reputation, quests, or a housing system.

## Completion Test

Belonging succeeds when the player understands:

- Their shelter was offered because the village chose to make room for them.
- They have somewhere in Willow Village to return to.
- The Guard and Chief remember their presence.
- Helping residents makes the player's place in village life feel more natural.
