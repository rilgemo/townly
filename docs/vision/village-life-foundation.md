# Village Life Foundation

## Player Experience

The player should understand that Willow Village was struggling before their arrival, but it was never dead.

Residents have spent years maintaining a modest continuity: tending small gardens, preserving supplies, repairing old tools, sharing what little is available, and remembering knowledge that can no longer be practiced fully.

The player is not rescuing people who waited motionless for a protagonist. They are joining people who have already worked hard to keep their home alive.

## World Change

This milestone does not simulate new activity. It makes existing life visible:

- cooking smoke rises from occupied homes
- small personal gardens grow between aging buildings
- old tools continue to be repaired and passed between neighbors
- limited stored supplies are managed carefully
- residents help one another when individual households fall short

Restoration strengthens this life. It does not create village life from nothing.

## How Willow Village Survives

Willow Village currently survives through four simple foundations.

### Stored Supplies

The village still has limited preserved food, fuel, material, and household goods accumulated over better years. These stores are finite and carefully managed; this milestone does not create inventory or economy systems for them.

### Small Gardens

Residents maintain personal garden plots sufficient to supplement their needs. These gardens establish continuity without implementing farming, crops, seasons, or production simulation.

### Mutual Help

Neighbors mend, share, and redistribute what they can. Mutual help explains survival as a social practice rather than an invisible economic system.

### Preserved Knowledge

Older residents remember work the village can no longer sustain at full scale. Their knowledge keeps lost possibilities understandable:

- The Village Chief preserves records, shared stores, and collective memory.
- The Old Woodsman remembers forest paths, usable wood, and the village's former fuel supply.
- The Former Miner remembers the tunnels, stonework, and the people who built with it.

They are not profession systems or production units. They preserve continuity between Willow Village's past and a possible future.

## Restoration Relationship

Restoration means helping existing life become less fragile.

The repaired Town Hall door matters because people still use the hall. A future workshop matters because residents still need tools. A recovered farm matters because small gardens already reveal how carefully food is maintained.

Every restoration should answer:

> What existing part of village life becomes stronger, safer, or more hopeful?

## Completion Test

Village Life Foundation succeeds when:

- Town Square visibly suggests occupied homes and daily effort.
- The village feels poor and aging rather than empty or abandoned.
- Existing residents express roles rooted in memory and continuity.
- The player understands stored supplies, gardens, mutual help, and knowledge as survival foundations.
- No farming, crafting, economy, inventory, time, schedule, relationship, or new resource system is introduced.
